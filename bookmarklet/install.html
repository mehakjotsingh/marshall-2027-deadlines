12twenty export

  1. Show the bookmarks bar: ⌘⇧B
  2. Drag this button onto the bookmarks bar (dragging, not clicking, is what installs it):

    Export 12twenty
  3. Open 12twenty and sign in.
  4. Click the bookmark. It fetches every open posting and saves 12twenty-export.json to Downloads.

A watcher picks the file up automatically, screens it, and publishes the calendar. You do not need to do anything else.

This runs entirely in your own signed-in browser using the session you already have. It stores no credentials and works around no protections — which is exactly why the fetch has to be a click you make, rather than something scheduled.
